## Design Context

### Users
CareerSense is used by students, freshers, job seekers, working professionals, and colleges. Users are trying to improve resumes, ATS readiness, interview confidence, certifications, and placement outcomes on both desktop and mobile.

### Brand Personality
Confident, modern, trustworthy.

### Aesthetic Direction
Clean career-tech interface with deep navy surfaces, bright blue actions, teal support accents, and strong white-card contrast. Mobile should feel like an intentional touch-first version of the same premium product, not a shrunk desktop page.

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize clarity and confidence over decoration.
2. Keep important actions obvious and thumb-friendly on mobile.
3. Preserve the premium dark-blue CareerSense identity across breakpoints.
4. Reflow and simplify layouts instead of only scaling them down.
5. Make key workflows easy to scan quickly on smaller screens.
